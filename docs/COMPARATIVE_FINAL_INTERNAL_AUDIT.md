# Comparative Final Internal Audit

`Stage 2-M4` closes the comparative layer internally by emitting a runtime audit,
not by writing a narrative verdict.

The audit checks at least:

- Stage 2 governance integrity;
- Stage 2-M2 runtime integrity;
- Stage 2-M3 contract-runtime integrity;
- taxonomy coherence across pathways, counterpart contracts, homologation, transition contracts, closure states, and Paper 10 gating;
- burden coherence and explicit open burdens;
- readiness coherence across M2, M3, and M4;
- surface discipline, path-leak discipline, and anti-overclaim discipline;
- cross-layer lineage freshness against the current local repo state.

Primary runtime artifact:

- `artifacts/comparative_program/stage2m4/comparative_final_internal_audit.json`

This audit remains framework-internal only. It does not open external
adjudication, does not authorize equivalence, and does not authorize Paper 10.
