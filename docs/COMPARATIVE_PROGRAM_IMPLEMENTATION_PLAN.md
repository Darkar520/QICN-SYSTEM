# Comparative Program Implementation Plan

## Role of Stage 2 and the readiness fulfillment pass

The comparative program follows `BPF-M3` internal bridge closure. Its job is to
prepare, bound, and eventually open a later comparative execution program
without fabricating human evidence and without silently promoting any claim.

The stack now consists of:

- `Stage2-0`: comparative opening and governance
- `Stage2-M2`: runtime comparative opening
- `Stage2-M3`: comparative operationalization
- `Stage2-M4`: comparative final internal closure
- `Stage3-RF`: Stage 3 readiness fulfillment

Legacy phase aliases kept for verifier continuity:

- `Stage2-3` = `Stage2-M3`
- `Stage2-4` = `Stage2-M4`
- `Stage2-5` = `Stage3-RF` burden-fulfillment and Paper 9 handoff hardening
- `Stage2-6` = `Stage3-RF` bounded Stage 3 opening gate emission

`Stage3-RF` is still not Stage 3 execution, not external adjudication, and not
Paper 10. It closes the remaining structural comparative burdens, hardens the
Paper 9 handoff substrate, and emits the bounded gate that decides whether Stage
3 may now open as an internal instantiation program.

## Stage3-RF: Stage 3 readiness fulfillment

- objective
  - fulfill the remaining Stage 3 structural burdens without fabricating human evidence.
  - bind human-channel instantiation protocols, counterpart schemas, comparative error budgets, and transport-side lineage contracts.
  - emit `comparative_burden_fulfillment.json`, `stage3_opening_readiness.json`, `paper9_handoff_alignment.json`, and `stage3_opening_gate.json`.
  - perform a second hardening pass on the local Paper 9-facing handoff substrate.
- closes
  - the remaining Stage 3 structural burdens as contract/schema/budget obligations.
  - the local Paper 9 handoff alignment pass.
  - the bounded decision over whether Stage 3 may now open.
- does not close
  - external adjudication
  - human equivalence
  - superiority or inferiority
  - transport-preservation verdicts
  - identity-preservation claims
  - Paper 10

## Current state

`Stage2-0`, `Stage2-M2`, `Stage2-M3`, `Stage2-M4`, and `Stage3-RF` are now
implemented. The comparative program is internally closed, the remaining Stage 3
structural burdens are fulfilled for opening, and the current readiness class is
`stage3_ready_but_uninstantiated`. This means Stage 3 may open as a bounded
internal instantiation program, but it still begins from uninstantiated human
channels and does not authorize external adjudication or Paper 10.
