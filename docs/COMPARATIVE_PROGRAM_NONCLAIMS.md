# Comparative Program Non-Claims

`Stage 2-M2`, `Stage 2-M3`, `Stage 2-M4`, and `Stage3-RF` do not create:

- human-equivalence claims;
- human phenomenal-equivalence claims;
- superiority claims;
- inferiority claims;
- cross-substrate equivalence claims;
- identity-preservation claims;
- transport-preservation verdicts;
- external adjudication;
- Paper 10 authorization;
- comparative public claim packages.

They also do not permit:

- fabricated human data;
- simulated human measurements presented as evidence;
- machine self-report relabelled as human evidence;
- synthetic human intervention profiles or negative controls;
- candidate or audit comparative integration;
- external comparative adjudication outputs;
- Paper 10 manuscript files;
- public comparator outputs.

Even when `Stage3-RF` emits `ready_for_stage3_opening`, the result remains:

- a bounded internal opening permission;
- not a human-equivalence verdict;
- not a superiority verdict;
- not a transport-preservation verdict;
- not external adjudication;
- not Paper 10.
