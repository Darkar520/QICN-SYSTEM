# Cross-Substrate Expansion

## Purpose

Stage 2 opens the cross-substrate expansion program as a comparative transport and
preservation layer. It does not claim that transport preservation has been shown.
It does not claim that any substrate remap preserves equivalence, identity, or
human-relevant structure.

## Formal target

At this layer, a substrate transition is any formally specified remapping from one
implementation substrate to another that purports to preserve some subset of the
subjectivity-plus-bridge comparative burden stack.

The preservation axes declared here are:

- `self_index_integrity`
- `bridge_coordinate_distortion`
- `intervention_response_stability`
- `reducibility_profile_preservation`
- `artifact_lineage_continuity`

These are future burden axes, not achieved transport properties.

## Transport ladder

The transport ladder is:

- `transport_not_open`
  - no transport mapping contract exists.
- `structurally_preserving_candidate`
  - a bounded structural subset may be preservable under explicit budgets.
- `partially_preserving_candidate`
  - some axes may be preserved while others remain open or degraded.
- `fractured_transport`
  - at least one declared preservation burden is violated.
- `forked_transport`
  - one source lineage yields multiple descendants, invalidating simple one-to-one transport language.
- `unsupported_remap`
  - the remap lacks enough invariance structure to count as a transport candidate.
- `comparator_underdetermined_transport`
  - the transport program is live, but evidence and burden contracts remain too thin.

## Future burden families

The transport program will later require:

- explicit mapping contracts;
- structural and interventional error budgets;
- fracture detection rules;
- fork detection rules;
- lineage branch accounting;
- invariance burdens;
- typed transport artifacts.

## What does not follow

This opening does not authorize:

- cross-substrate equivalence claims;
- identity preservation claims;
- human transport claims;
- external adjudication claims.

## Current state

Cross-substrate expansion is now formally opened as a governance layer only. It
remains `defined_not_runtime_adjudicated`.
