# External Adjudication Opening Criteria

`Stage 2-M4` does not open external adjudication. It defines, in machine-readable
form, what must be true before `Stage 3` may open later.

Primary criteria manifest:

- `docs/external_adjudication_opening_criteria.v1.json`

The criteria require at least:

- legitimate human-channel instantiation routes;
- instantiated counterpart artifact families;
- homologation conditions beyond counterpart absence;
- intervention symmetry with matched controls;
- burden symmetry and error-budget binding;
- mature transition contracts;
- preserved surface discipline and anti-overclaim discipline.

This manifest is criteria-only. It does not authorize Stage 3 by itself, does
not instantiate human evidence, and does not authorize any public claim. A
scaffold-only Paper 10 source may now exist under `Stage3-0`, but this criteria
layer still forbids treating that scaffold as final adjudication, final Paper
10 authorization, or a public claim surface.
