# Human Channel Pathways

This document summarizes the Stage 2-M3 pathway manifest
`docs/human_channel_pathways.v1.json`.

## Role

Stage 2-M3 does not instantiate human channels. It operationalizes how a
legitimate human channel would have to be opened, what artifact family would be
required, and which substitutions remain forbidden.

## Pathway classes

The pathway layer now distinguishes:

- `pathway_not_open`
- `pathway_defined_but_uninstantiated`
- `pathway_contract_incomplete`
- `pathway_contract_ready_but_counterpart_absent`
- `pathway_blocked_by_forbidden_proxy`
- `pathway_theoretically_open`

## Pathway families

The current Stage 2-M3 pathway layer covers:

- self-report structural pathways;
- intervention-response pathways;
- temporal-structure pathways;
- valence-topology pathways;
- embodiment-boundary pathways;
- ambiguity-load pathways;
- transport-preservation pathways.

## Boundary

Pathway readiness is not human evidence. It is not equivalence support. It is
not external adjudication. It only records whether the future route is bounded,
what remains missing, and what must not be substituted.
