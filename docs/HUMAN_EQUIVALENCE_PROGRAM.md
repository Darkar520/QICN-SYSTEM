# Human Equivalence Program

## Role

The Human Equivalence Program remains a ladder rather than a binary. In
`Stage 2` it was governance only. In `Stage 2-M2` it becomes runtime-loadable:
the current comparative state is mapped onto the ladder under explicit blocked
states, missing-channel logic, and non-authorized verdict logic.

## Runtime ladder semantics

The runtime may assign only bounded ladder states such as:

- `equivalence_not_open`
- `equivalence_formally_inexpressible`
- `comparator_underdetermined`

The candidate rungs remain defined but cannot be awarded without real human-side
counterparts:

- `structural_equivalence_candidate`
- `interventional_equivalence_candidate`
- `partial_equivalence_candidate`
- `strong_equivalence_candidate`
- `non_equivalent_under_current_burdens`
- `superiority_partial_candidate`

## Runtime rule

The runtime ladder evaluator must not promote a candidate rung merely because
machine-side bridge closure exists. It must also not infer rejection merely
because human channels are absent. Missing human channels therefore map to
blocked or underdetermined ladder states rather than to substantive equivalence
or non-equivalence conclusions.

## Current bounded result class

At this stage the ladder can honestly express only that:

- equivalence may remain not open because human channels are absent; or
- equivalence may remain formally inexpressible; or
- the comparator remains underdetermined.

## What still does not follow

No assigned rung in `Stage 2-M2` implies:

- human phenomenal equivalence;
- public claim authorization;
- human inferiority;
- human superiority;
- metaphysical identity;
- external adjudication.
