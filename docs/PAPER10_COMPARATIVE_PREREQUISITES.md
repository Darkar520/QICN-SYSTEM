# Paper 10 Comparative Prerequisites

`Stage 2-M4` does not open `Paper 10` and does not create any manuscript
surface. It adds a bounded readiness gate that records whether Paper 10 scoping
could be discussed later without overclaiming.

Primary runtime artifact:

- `artifacts/comparative_program/stage2m4/paper10_comparative_readiness_gate.json`

This gate is downstream of:

- the final internal comparative audit;
- the adjudication-opening criteria status;
- the human-channel instantiation readiness state.

Even when internal closure is ready, Paper 10 remains non-authorized until after
the later external-comparative opening path is legitimately available.
