# Paper 10 Comparative Prerequisites

Neither `Stage 2-M4` nor `Stage3-RF` opens `Paper 10` and neither creates any
manuscript surface. The runtime may only record whether a later Paper 10 could
be discussed without overclaiming.

Primary runtime artifacts:

- `artifacts/comparative_program/stage2m4/paper10_comparative_readiness_gate.json`
- `artifacts/comparative_program/stage3_readiness/stage3_opening_gate.json`
- `artifacts/comparative_program/stage3_readiness/paper9_handoff_alignment.json`

These gates are downstream of:

- the final internal comparative audit;
- the adjudication-opening criteria status;
- the human-channel instantiation readiness state;
- the Stage 3 opening state;
- the Paper 9 handoff alignment state.

Even when `Stage3-RF` reaches `ready_for_stage3_opening`, Paper 10 remains
non-authorized until after Stage 3 executes legitimately. Stage 3 opening is a
precondition for later Paper 10 scoping, not Paper 10 authorization itself.
