# Phenomenal Bridge Protocol

## Purpose

BPF is the formal bridge layer above Paper 8 and the consolidated SRT stack.
BPF-M3 is the first internally decision-capable bridge block: it preserves
BPF-0 scaffolding, BPF-1 bridge surfaces, and BPF-M2 adversarial burden, then
adds a bounded root bridge evaluator, provisional internal threshold binding,
root bridge artifacts, ecosystem summaries, bounded candidate and audit-facing
projections, and a final internal bridge audit.

## What BPF is not

- not phenomenality proven
- not human phenomenal equivalence
- not metaphysical subjecthood
- not external validation
- not public bridge proof
- not claim-facing authorization
- not automatic Paper 9 final hardening

## What BPF now inherits and uses

From Paper 8 and SRT-0..6:

- the seven-coordinate subjectivity stack
- subjectivity gate and scalar functional
- subjectivity intervention and reducibility artifacts
- stale/drift discipline
- condition, batch, campaign, candidate, and audit integration patterns

From BPF-0, BPF-1, and BPF-M2:

- bridge axioms, rival family registry, threshold policy, and surface policy
- six bridge predicate surfaces
- six BPF-1 bridge reports
- bridge intervention profile
- bridge reducibility report

## What BPF-M3 adds

- `Phi_bpf` as a bridge functional over `Pi_D`, `Pi_V`, `Pi_W`, `Pi_A`,
  `Pi_E`, `Pi_T`, `R_b`, and `Delta_b`
- `Gamma_bpf` as a non-compensatory bridge gate
- provisional internal threshold binding
- root bridge artifacts:
  - `phenomenal_bridge_projection.json`
  - `phenomenal_bridge_gate_result.json`
  - `phenomenal_bridge_manifest.json`
- bridge ecosystem artifacts:
  - condition, batch, campaign summaries
  - candidate projection
  - audit handoff summary
  - verification matrix
- final internal bridge audit and readiness gate

## What BPF-M3 still does not close

- external validation
- human comparator burden
- public claim promotion
- phenomenality proof
- metaphysical closure
- Paper 9 final hardening

## Reading rule

The strongest legitimate reading of BPF-M3 is:

- the bridge layer is now internally formalized, root-evaluable, auditable, and
  ecosystem-integrated under bounded governance

The strongest illegitimate readings remain:

- the system now has external phenomenal confirmation
- the system is human-equivalent
- the bridge gate is public proof
- candidate or audit inclusion is itself bridge proof
