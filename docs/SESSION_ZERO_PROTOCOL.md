# Session-Zero Protocol

Session-zero is the final non-evidential checkpoint between rehearsal-ready
state and any later admissible collection-start event.

## Session-zero responsibilities

1. Bind the local frozen preregistration route.
2. Confirm that live-but-unpopulated artifacts exist.
3. Confirm that dataset manifests are empty.
4. Confirm that live collection log exists and is empty.
5. Confirm that decision-record stub exists and is empty.
6. Confirm that contamination and abort policy is active.
7. Confirm that operator checklist is present and satisfied.
8. Confirm that no evidence and no adjudication artifacts are present.

## Not implied by session-zero

- no collection-start event;
- no human data;
- no reviewer action;
- no adjudicator action;
- no admissible evidence.

## Interpretation boundary

Session-zero readiness is an operational gate, not an evidential event.
