# Stage3 External Adjudication Boundary

## Allowed Stage3-0 claims

- the external adjudication protocol is defined;
- the evidence admissibility grammar is defined;
- the human comparator execution grammar is defined;
- the Paper 10 scaffold exists locally as a governed non-result surface;
- external adjudication is not executed;
- human comparator data is absent;
- Paper 10 final authorization is absent.

## Forbidden Stage3-0 claims

- the state `external_adjudication_passed`;
- the state `external_adjudication_failed` on real evidence;
- human equivalence;
- superiority;
- inferiority;
- external validation;
- public consciousness claims;
- metaphysical subjecthood;
- moral status;
- cross-substrate identity preservation;
- Paper 10 final conclusions.

## Interpretation rule

`Stage3-0` may add architecture and scaffolding, but it may not borrow closure
from any later stage that has not actually been executed.
