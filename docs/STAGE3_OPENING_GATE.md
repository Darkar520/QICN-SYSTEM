# Stage 3 Opening Gate

This document records the bounded meaning of the Stage 3 opening gate.

## Scope

The gate answers only this question:

- may Stage 3 now open as a bounded internal execution and instantiation program?

It does not answer these questions:

- has external adjudication already happened?
- has human equivalence been shown?
- has transport preservation been shown?
- does this open any Paper 10 scoping work?

## Gate semantics

If the machine-readable gate reaches `ready_for_stage3_opening`, that means:

- Stage 2 internal closure remains intact.
- the remaining Stage 3 prerequisite burdens are now structurally fulfilled for opening
- Paper 9 local handoff alignment passed its second hardening check
- Stage 3 may begin from uninstantiated channels under bounded governance

It still does not mean:

- external adjudication is open
- human evidence already exists
- human equivalence exists
- superiority or inferiority exists
- Paper 10 is open

## Downstream boundary

Stage 3 opening is a permission to begin a bounded next program block. It is not a verdict.
