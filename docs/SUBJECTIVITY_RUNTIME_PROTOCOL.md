## Reading rule

`Gamma_subj` and `Phi_subj` are internal runtime objects only. A passing
internal gate means only that the currently implemented SRT-5 artifacts satisfy
the provisional internal burden under clean comparator support, intervention
stability, and no drift mismatch. It does not mean validated subjectivity in
the external or phenomenal sense.
