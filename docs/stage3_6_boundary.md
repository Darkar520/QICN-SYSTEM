# Stage3-6 Controlled Execution Boundary

Stage3-6 opens a bounded controlled-execution surface only.

Allowed:

- live-but-unpopulated artifact instantiation;
- session-zero manifest creation;
- empty live collection log creation;
- pre-collection admissibility gating;
- contamination, abort, and invalidation policy binding;
- collection-openable-under-controls and collection-open-pre-evidential grammar.

Still blocked:

- collection-start events;
- populated datasets;
- evidence generation;
- reviewer decisions;
- adjudicator decisions;
- adjudication results;
- Paper 10 final authorization;
- human-equivalence, superiority, inferiority, identity-preservation, or metaphysical claims.

Interpretation rule:

Stage3-6 may instantiate live operational shells and open a controlled
pre-evidential state. It may not treat those shells as evidence, collection,
or adjudication.
