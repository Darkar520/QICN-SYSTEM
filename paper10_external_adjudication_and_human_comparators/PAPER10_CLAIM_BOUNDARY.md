# Paper 10 Claim Boundary

## Allowed now

- protocol architecture;
- instantiation contracts;
- preregistration templates;
- reviewer and adjudicator independence architecture;
- evidence admissibility grammar;
- matched intervention and negative-control dataset contracts;
- statistical shells;
- blocked result placeholders;
- readiness ledgers;
- explicit non-claim and forbidden-claim surfaces.

## Blocked until execution

- human comparator result statements;
- dataset-sensitive comparative statements;
- execution-era effect descriptions;
- any statement that depends on actual frozen preregistration records;
- any statement that depends on actual reviewer selection or adjudicator assignment.

## Blocked until external adjudication

- adjudication result statements;
- evidence-weighting statements attributed to independent reviewers;
- any statement that requires conflict disclosure packets or blinding logs.

## Forbidden now

- human equivalence;
- superiority or inferiority;
- consciousness claims;
- phenomenality closure claims;
- moral status;
- metaphysical subjecthood;
- cross-substrate identity preservation;
- Paper 10 final conclusions;
- public release recommendation.

## Still forbidden even after a future successful execution unless separate burdens are met

- identity-preservation claims;
- metaphysical consequence claims;
- moral-status claims;
- any collapse of operational comparator similarity into ontological equivalence.

## Interpretation rule

Paper 10 may become denser, more structured, and more operationally precise.
It may not become more conclusive until admissible execution-era evidence exists
and passes the later reviewer-governed adjudication pathway.
