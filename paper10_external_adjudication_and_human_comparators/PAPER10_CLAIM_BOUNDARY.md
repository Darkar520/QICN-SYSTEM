# Paper 10 Claim Boundary

## Allowed now

- dependency architecture;
- release-vs-local corpus boundary semantics;
- lineage and provenance semantics;
- provenance closure semantics;
- instantiation contracts;
- preregistration freeze architecture;
- instantiated-empty record semantics;
- reviewer and adjudicator candidate architecture;
- candidate-pool and assignment-stub grammar;
- reviewer rejection logic;
- blinding assignment logic;
- execution pilot package architecture;
- rehearsal-kit architecture;
- controlled execution opening architecture;
- live-but-unpopulated artifact architecture;
- session-zero protocol;
- pre-collection admissibility grammar;
- contamination, abort, and invalidation grammar;
- operator-checklist grammar;
- dry-run event-log grammar;
- execution state grammar;
- reproducibility skeleton architecture;
- evidence admissibility grammar;
- statistical shells;
- decision grammar as grammar only;
- blocked result placeholders;
- readiness ledgers;
- explicit non-claim and forbidden-claim surfaces.

## Blocked until execution

- human comparator result statements;
- dataset-sensitive comparative statements;
- execution-era effect descriptions;
- any statement that depends on actual frozen preregistration locks;
- any statement that depends on actual candidate-pool population;
- any statement that depends on actual reviewer selection or adjudicator assignment;
- any statement that depends on actual blinding assignment;
- any statement that depends on actual collection sessions or populated logs;
- any statement that depends on populated pilot bundles.
- any statement that depends on collection-start events;
- any statement that depends on live artifact population;
- any statement that depends on decision-record population.

## Blocked until external adjudication

- adjudication result statements;
- evidence-weighting statements attributed to independent reviewers;
- any statement that requires conflict disclosure packets or blinding logs;
- any statement that requires an adjudicator decision record.
- any statement that requires instantiated-empty grammar to be treated as evidence.

## Forbidden now

- human equivalence;
- superiority or inferiority;
- consciousness claims;
- phenomenality closure claims;
- moral status;
- metaphysical subjecthood;
- cross-substrate identity preservation;
- Paper 10 final conclusions;
- public release recommendation.

## Still forbidden even after a future successful pilot or execution unless separate burdens are met

- identity-preservation claims;
- metaphysical consequence claims;
- moral-status claims;
- any collapse of operational comparator similarity into ontological equivalence;
- any collapse of dry-run success into evidential support.

## Interpretation rule

Paper 10 may become denser, more structured, and more operationally precise.
It may not become more conclusive until admissible execution-era evidence exists,
reviewer-governed adjudication exists, and the later result-bearing sections are
honestly written from those artifacts.
Controlled execution opening remains a pre-evidential state only; it is not a
license to speak as if collection had already started.
