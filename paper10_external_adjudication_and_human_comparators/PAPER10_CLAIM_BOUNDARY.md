# Paper 10 Claim Boundary

## Allowed at Stage3-0

- protocol architecture;
- evidence admissibility grammar;
- comparator-channel definitions;
- preregistration burdens;
- reviewer independence criteria;
- negative-control design;
- blocked result placeholders;
- readiness and non-claim ledgers.

## Forbidden at Stage3-0

- external adjudication verdicts;
- human comparator results;
- human equivalence;
- superiority or inferiority;
- consciousness claims;
- moral status;
- metaphysical subjecthood;
- cross-substrate identity preservation;
- Paper 10 final conclusions.

## Interpretation rule

The scaffold may become more detailed, but not more conclusive, until
admissible external evidence exists.
